# Professionals who work inside the university

__Dr Gareth Tribello__ is a senior lecturer in the school of mathematics and physics at Queens University Belfast.  He specialises in computational chemistry, materials science and atomistic simulation, particularly with the Centre for Quantumaterial Materials and Techonolgies.  He is one of the core project leaders and developers of [PLUMED](www.plumed.org), a widely used open-source plugin for enhanced sampling, free-energy calculations, and analyzing molecular dynamics simulations

__Prof John Barry__ is a professor of green political economy at Queen's University Belfast and one of the co-directors for the [Centre for Sustainability, Equality and Climate Action (SECA)](https://www.qub.ac.uk/research-centres/SECA/). His core research challenges conventional economics. He specializes in post-growth and heterodox political economy, green political theory, just energy transitions, and analyzing why modern capitalistic growth is fundamentally incompatible with a finite planetary ecology. He was also a core driving force behind the formation of the all-island Green Party and previously served as the joint leader of the Green Party in Northern Ireland.

__Dr Calum McGeown__ is an interdisciplinary climate justice researcher and political theorist whose work focuses on radical climate activism, post-growth political economy, and the role of higher education in the planetary crisis. Dr McGeown’s research heavily critiques traditional "green growth" and techno-optimistic climate solutions under neoliberal capitalism. He explores eco-socialism, green republicanism, and counter-hegemonic political theories to frame how society can transition to a post-carbon, post-capitalist world.

Holding the role since 2018, he is highly regarded for his vocal advocacy for rural accessibility and transport equity.
