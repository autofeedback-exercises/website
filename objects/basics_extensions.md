# Extension Projects: Basics of manipulating sets

In the example report I examined the behaviour of the solutions of equations of the type:

$$
z^n-1=0
$$

You can extend on this work by doing a similar investigation for an equation such as:

$$
z^n - r = 0
$$

You can choose whatever value you like for $r$ and examine if there are still shared roots.  
You can take this even further by explorin multiple different $r$ values.
